# Audit B — Buildings and Facilities

## B1 Inventory and taxonomy
Primary functions: Access, Stability, Conversion, Specialization. Families: core/community, infrastructure, production, combat/support, social/adult, transformative/magical, narrative landmarks.

## B2 Functional rule
A standalone building must introduce a distinct player choice. Otherwise use an upgrade, module, room, event unlock, or visual variation.

## B3 Adult integration
Taverns, intimacy venues/brothels, BDSM spaces, sensory gardens, aphrodisiac environments, and transformation facilities use the same four-function architecture; no separate adult economy or management subsystem.

## B4 Progression architecture
Buildings may be unlocked by narrative milestones, region discoveries, community growth, or specialized prerequisites. Prefer a small number of meaningful upgrade tiers. Construction should usually expand options rather than provide linear percentage inflation.

## Findings
PASS: A9 resource architecture provides sufficient building roles.

## Pending decisions
Exact initial roster; independent building vs module for adult venues; visual spatial representation; upgrade-tier density; specific construction gates.
