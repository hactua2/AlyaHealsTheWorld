# Audit C — Items, Equipment, Inventory, and Transmutation

## C1 Taxonomy
Resources, components, consumables, equipment, narrative/unique items. Avoid decorative mechanical categories with no rules difference.

## C2 Inventory
Stack recurring resources/components/consumables. Keep equipment and unique narrative objects individually identifiable. Do not add weight/encumbrance unless a later gameplay need proves it valuable.

## C3 Equipment
Equipment must alter choices, roles, skill interactions, defenses, or access to capabilities; avoid a primarily linear stat ladder. Stats remain governed by the established source-of-truth rule. Removing equipment removes equipment-derived effects immediately.

## C4 Transmutation
One modification system, not several parallel enhancement trees. Arcane and Organic Components remain the principal structural inputs. Transmutation should offer specialization/trade-offs and controlled empowerment, not merely repeated +X upgrades.

## Pending decisions
Slot architecture; duplicate-item policy; consumable families; transmutation permanence/reversibility; exact modification limits; acquisition gates for advanced equipment.
