# Audit I — Narrative × Gameplay Integration

## Core test
The repeated player actions must materially express Alya's effort to create conditions for harmony rather than contradicting it through unrelated optimization loops.

## Narrative-compatible gameplay verbs
Explore, encounter, understand, negotiate, care for, transform, confront, integrate, build, assign, specialize, and restore/redirect relationships.

## Wound integration
The Wound changes relationships, ecologies, characters, and opportunities. Gameplay responses can include combat, cooperation, care, transformation, investigation, or community action. Avoid making corruption equivalent to non-consensual sexualization.

## Adult integration
Sexuality is material, cultural, magical, biological, and narrative. Adult content can be a reward, relationship, environment, quest context, building function, or encounter resolution, but should not displace all other motivations.

## Ending compatibility
Because final harmony is a long-term condition rather than a fully measurable instant outcome, player feedback should communicate direction/condition rather than a deterministic universal percentage.

## Pending decisions
Exact relationship between visible harmony feedback and hidden consequences; how frequently player actions explicitly affect the world state; presentation of moral/relational ambiguity.
