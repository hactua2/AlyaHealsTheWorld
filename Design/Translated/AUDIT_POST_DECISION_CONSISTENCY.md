# Post-Decision Consistency Audit

## Scope
Validate the final decisions against the Skills Audit, resource/ecology architecture A1–A10, audits B–J, and the consolidated Architecture Source of Truth.

## Finding 1 — Main-quest gating and renewable resources
Status: PASS.
Renewability prevents permanent economic collapse; narrative and regional gates control access to new possibility layers. These solve different problems and do not conflict.

## Finding 2 — Visible missing resources and mystery
Status: PASS.
Visible systemic requirements plus later-unlocked regions support player planning. Narrative discoveries can remain mysterious. This is compatible with the hybrid gate-visibility policy.

## Finding 3 — Base optimization versus story pacing
Status: PASS.
Players may optimize the current layer, but advanced upgrades, facilities, resources, and regions remain story-gated. This directly addresses indefinite pre-story community development.

## Finding 4 — Procedural exploration and region architecture
Status: PASS.
A8 defines productive identities rather than fixed maps, so procedural generation can instantiate those identities. Hand-authored maps remain available for story-critical content.

## Finding 5 — Retreat versus narrative consequences
Status: PASS.
Contextual opportunity loss and changed future conditions fit the anti-punitive ecological and narrative principles better than universal game over.

## Finding 6 — Aptitude retention
Status: PASS.
Permanent acquired experience reinforces experimentation and prevents assignment management from becoming punitive. Natural aptitude can still differentiate characters through learning speed, efficiency, reliability, or access without causing skill decay.

## Finding 7 — Equipment experimentation
Status: PASS.
Few impactful slots, reversible transmutation, evolvable equipment, and facility-based light-cost respec all support build experimentation while preserving meaningful choices.

## Finding 8 — Gold and material economy
Status: PASS WITH GUARDRAIL.
Gold is a conventional purchasing currency; specialized resources remain required for structural, magical, ecological, equipment, and transformation decisions. Do not let Gold universally substitute for gated materials or Unique Resources.

## Finding 9 — Quest reward previews
Status: PASS.
Reward hints improve intentional progression without requiring complete spoiler disclosure. Major rewards can be previewed, while hidden twists, alternate outcomes, and secondary discoveries remain undisclosed.

## Finding 10 — Harmony feedback and unknowable final outcome
Status: PASS.
A qualitative global trajectory plus visible regional consequences gives useful feedback without reducing the long-term ending to a deterministic percentage.

## Finding 11 — Adult integration
Status: PASS.
Standalone adult facilities only when mechanically meaningful; otherwise modules or extensions. This strengthens sexualization while controlling building and system proliferation.

## Finding 12 — Enemy scaling
Status: PASS.
Fixed/content-authored bands plus behavioral variation preserve authored identity and prevent automatic scaling from erasing progression. Main-quest gates also limit access to inappropriate difficulty layers.

## Finding 13 — Remaining architectural contradictions
None found that require another macro-system or immediate decision batch.

## Remaining implementation-level questions
These are concrete design tasks rather than architectural blockers:
- exact initial building roster and module distribution
- exact equipment slots and specialized-slot unlocks
- transmutation UI and configuration limits
- Gold sources and sinks
- concrete region roster and procedural-generation parameters
- exact gate schedule by act/main-quest milestone
- assignment capacity, automation, and community-size rules
- party composition outside combat
- specific quest reward previews
- numeric balance and economy

## Final Verdict
ARCHITECTURE CONSISTENT.

The decision backlog identified by audit J is resolved at policy level. Remaining work is primarily content definition, implementation specification, balancing, and presentation. New systems should require explicit justification rather than being assumed from missing concrete content.
