# Audit F — Progression and Gating

## F1 Core principle
Narrative progress is the primary gate for new possibility layers. Base development, exploration, equipment, and resources reinforce the story rather than replacing it as the dominant progression path.

## F2 Gate types
Hard gates protect major narrative/systemic milestones. Soft gates allow early contact or experimentation without full exploitation. Prefer soft gates for curiosity and hard gates for systems that would destabilize pacing.

## F3 Anti-overdevelopment
Before a main-quest milestone, recurring systems may have local caps, diminishing returns, locked advanced tiers, unavailable assignments, region restrictions, or conversion bottlenecks. Do not rely on arbitrary global time limits.

## Progression loop
Story milestone -> new possibility -> community/base exploitation -> new capabilities -> next story pressure/opportunity.

## Pending decisions
Exact gate implementation mix; how visible gates should be; whether story milestones cap stockpiling or only spending/upgrades; failure/alternative-path policy; pacing between acts.
