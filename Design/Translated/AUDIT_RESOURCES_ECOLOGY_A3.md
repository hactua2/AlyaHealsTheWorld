# Resource & Ecology Audit A3 — Initial Resource Set

## Decision
Adopt a deliberately small recurring resource architecture. The target is not a complete catalogue of world objects, but the minimum set of systemic resources required by the existing loops.

## Core Recurring Resources

### Common Materials
1. **Timber** — construction, repair, furniture, structural uses. Trait example: Botanical.
2. **Stone** — foundations, permanent structures, installations. Variants may later carry additional traits.
3. **Metal** — equipment, tools, structures, mechanisms. Specific metals remain content variants rather than separate systemic currencies initially.
4. **Fiber** — textiles, rope, flexible materials, clothing, equipment, furniture, and construction uses.

### Special Materials
5. **Essence** — broad biological, magical, or vital specialized material used for advanced production, upgrades, items, buildings, and transformation.
6. **Nectar** — primarily botanical and sensory material supporting production, alchemy, consumption, transformation, and specialized content.
7. **Musk** — broad family of sensory and biological compounds derived from creatures, plants, alchemy, or magical phenomena; supports specialized products, buildings, events, and items.
8. **Estrus** — biological or energetic material associated with fertility, desire, vitality, cycles, and transformation; may be collected, cultivated, condensed, magically generated, or produced by fantastical organisms.

### Components
9. **Arcane Component** — dedicated Transmutation input with possible Magical, Transformative, or Wound-Touched traits.
10. **Organic Component** — biological counterpart used for Transmutation and specialized items or equipment.

### Unique Resources
11. **Individualized Unique Resources** — named regional, quest-related, narrative, magical, or Wound-derived resources. These are content-specific rather than a single generic item.

## Explicit Decision: Aphrodisiac Flora
Do not add a generic recurring resource named Aphrodisiac Flora. Sexualized and aphrodisiac plants should exist as world content and may produce Nectar, Essence, Musk, Components, or Unique Resources. This preserves strong ecological identity without adding another systemic currency.

## Functional Identities
- Essence: power, vitality, specialization.
- Nectar: botany, cultivation, consumption.
- Musk: sensory influence and specialized products.
- Estrus: fertility, cycles, transformation.

## Ferida Rule
Do not introduce a dedicated recurring Wound Material at this stage. The Wound may alter existing resources through traits or produce exceptional Unique Resources when narratively justified.

## Scope Guardrails
- No separate NSFW economy.
- No separate sexual inventory.
- No separate sexual crafting loop.
- No full farming simulator required.
- Specific variants should remain content unless they require distinct mechanical behavior.

## Resulting Architecture
Recurring systemic resources: 10 named base resources plus individualized Unique Resources.

The next audit, A4, maps each resource to sources and acquisition chains across Exploration, Production, Expeditions, Quests, Encounters, processing, and existing Transmutation loops while minimizing grind and redundant acquisition paths.
