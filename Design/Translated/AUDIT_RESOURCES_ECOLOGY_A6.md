# Resource & Ecology Audit A6 — Minimum Productive Ecology

## Purpose
Define the minimum ecological structure capable of grounding approved resources in the world without creating a detailed biome simulator, farming simulator, or complete food-web model.

## Core Productive Groups
1. Flora
2. Fauna and other organisms
3. Phenomena and environments
4. Community production as managed/domesticated ecology

The Wound is a modifier of existing ecology rather than a separate economic ecosystem.

## Flora Roles

### Structural Flora
Primary outputs: Timber and Fiber.
Examples may include trees, vines, resilient vegetation, structural fungi, and fantastical equivalents.

### Sensory Flora
Primary outputs: Nectar and Musk, with occasional Essence.
Includes aromatic, pollen-producing, resinous, secretory, and sensorial organisms.

### Transformative Flora
Primary outputs: Essence, Nectar, Organic Components, and selected Unique Resources.
Associated with metamorphosis, fertility, development, adaptation, and magic.

### Exceptional Flora
Content-level category primarily associated with Unique Resources, quests, regional discoveries, and narrative landmarks.

## Fauna and Organism Roles

### Producers
Naturally generate or enable acquisition of Essence, Musk, Estrus, and organic materials through cooperation, care, exchange, natural collection, biological cycles, or events. Resources should not be assumed to require violence.

### Structural Organisms
Provide Fiber, Organic Components, or equipment materials through shedding, growth, exchange, or other non-destructive interactions where appropriate.

### Magical Organisms
Connect living ecology to Essence, Arcane Components, and Unique Resources.

### Exceptional Organisms
Quest, regional, character, or narrative-specific sources of Unique Resources.

## Environmental Roles

### Mineral Environments
Support Stone and Metal.

### Magical Phenomena
Support Essence, Arcane Components, and Unique Resources.

### Sensory Environments
Support Nectar, Musk, Essence, and Estrus through the ecology or conditions of the location.

## Community Production
Base production represents managed ecology rather than abstract resource generation:
- cultivate sensory flora -> Nectar
- maintain magical organisms -> Essence
- process naturally shed biological material -> Organic Components
- care for productive organisms -> Musk / Estrus

These activities remain abstracted through existing community assignment and production systems; no standalone farming simulator is required.

## Wound Rule
The Wound modifies organisms, environments, or existing resources. It creates a separate item only when a distinct mechanical or narrative function requires one; otherwise use existing resources with Wound-Touched or related traits.

## Deferred Scope
Do not define yet:
- complete biome catalogue
- individual species catalogue
- food chains
- seasons and seasonal simulation
- simulated reproduction systems
- genetics
- detailed agriculture
- ecological maps

## Result
The world has a minimum productive ecology capable of supporting resources, exploration, encounters, expeditions, and community production while remaining compatible with strong sexualization of flora, fauna, environments, and material culture.

The next audit, A7, defines renewal, pressure, scarcity, and sustainability rules for these resources and ecological sources.
