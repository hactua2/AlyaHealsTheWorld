# Narrative Structure

## Narrative contract

The implementation-facing narrative is a traditional fantasy story about resistance, community formation, regional liberation, and the growth of a shared base.

Specific presentation details may change, but the following structural beats are preserved.

## Regional act pattern

Each major region may contain:

1. Arrival and orientation.
2. A local conflict.
3. Discovery of the wider cause or opposing force.
4. Introduction of new gameplay or community possibilities.
5. Main quest escalation.
6. Optional quests and recruitable opportunities.
7. A major confrontation.
8. Regional resolution.
9. A change to the base, roster, or player capabilities.
10. A transition hook toward the next region.

## Character narrative roles

Characters may function as allies, neutral actors, quest givers, quest targets, conditional allies, opponents, or surrendered combatants. These roles are data and state driven.

## Surrender narrative flow

1. Combat resolves through Moral depletion.
2. The target enters Surrender.
3. Combat ends or transitions according to encounter rules.
4. A post-combat dialogue/event evaluates authored conditions.
5. The event may offer recruitment, quest progress, release, refusal, or another authored outcome.
6. Recruitment changes roster availability only when the event explicitly grants it.

## Progression principle

Narrative progression and base progression should reinforce one another: resolving regional conflicts creates new relationships or capabilities, while community growth creates new options for future exploration.
