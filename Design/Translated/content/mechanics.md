# Translated Mechanics Content

## Source-derived systems

This document translates the concrete mechanics roster without inventing balancing rules.

### Integration

**Source role:** Village system.

Neutral contract:

- an enemy or hostile unit can enter an integration pipeline;
- integration occurs through a dedicated facility or process;
- the process may alter unit statistics or state;
- each unit may have distinct strengths and weaknesses relevant to the process;
- successful resolution may make a unit available as an ally.

The source does not define costs, duration, capacity, success formulas, or which units are eligible.

### Resource Production

**Source role:** Village system.

Neutral contract:

- the base can produce or obtain resources through an ongoing production activity.

No canonical production categories, rates, costs, or worker assignments are defined by the source.

### Transmutation

**Source role:** Village system.

Neutral contract:

- components obtained through village production can be consumed to empower equipment.

The source does not define recipes, output tiers, probabilities, or exact upgrade formulas.

### Expedition Dispatch

**Source role:** Village system.

Neutral contract:

- allies can be assigned to adventures/expeditions;
- expeditions can return rare resources;
- expeditions can occasionally result in additional allies becoming available;
- ally acquisition can occur through integration or another authored route.

No canonical duration, risk, success formula, party size, or failure state is defined by the source.

### Persistent Affliction Progression

**Source role:** Character system.

Neutral contract:

- characters can possess persistent states or afflictions;
- those states can progress over time or according to defined triggers.

The source does not define progression stages or triggers.

## Relationship to Moral and Surrender

The project owner established the canonical flow:

`Moral defeat -> Surrender -> post-combat narrative resolution`

Integration is a separate system and is not synonymous with Surrender or automatic recruitment.
