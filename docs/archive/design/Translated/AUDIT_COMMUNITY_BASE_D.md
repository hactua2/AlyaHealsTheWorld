# Audit D — Community, Assignments, and Base Management

## D1 Member lifecycle
Recruit/Join -> Integrate -> Assign -> Gain aptitude through time and experience -> Unlock specialization/opportunities -> Personal and narrative development. Natural aptitude accelerates learning but does not make other characters invalid.

## D2 Assignment architecture
Use broad work domains rather than a huge profession catalogue: production/ecology, expedition/exploration support, care/recovery, research/transmutation, combat/training, and social/specialized services. A member can change assignments; aptitude develops gradually and should not become a grind target.

## D3 Community as content
Members are characters, not passive +% modifiers. Assignments can create events, relationships, discoveries, conflicts, stories, and opportunities. Adult/social roles follow the same character-first principle.

## Base loop
Narrative progress unlocks possibilities; community assignments stabilize or specialize them; resulting growth supports further narrative progress.

## Pending decisions
Assignment capacity/concurrency; respec or aptitude decay; automation level; whether aptitude is visible as rank, descriptor, or both; community size pressure/caps.
