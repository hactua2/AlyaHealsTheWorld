# Audit E — Exploration, Encounters, and Regions

## E1 Exploration loop
Enter region -> choose activity/path -> encounter/discovery -> resolve -> reward/change -> continue or return. Exploration should not be a pure resource-farming screen.

## E2 Encounter taxonomy
Combat; social; ecological; adult/intimate; narrative; discovery; danger; opportunity. Encounters may combine categories. Adult content is contextual and consent-oriented rather than a separate encounter engine.

## E3 Region architecture
Use A8 productive profiles plus narrative state. A concrete region normally has one primary productive identity, optional secondary influence, and optional Wound modifier. Regions can change state after quests/events.

## Repeatability
Unique story encounters resolve permanently or transform. Repeatable activities use variable states/reward pools to avoid identical farming.

## Pending decisions
Exploration presentation (node/map/free-roam/hybrid); party size outside combat; retreat/failure consequences; fog/discovery model; exact repeatability rules.
