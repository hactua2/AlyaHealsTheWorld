# Resource & Ecology Audit A10 — Consolidation and Consistency Test

## Purpose
Perform a final consistency pass across A1–A9 and identify unresolved contradictions, orphaned resources, redundant loops, and scope-creep risks.

## Final Architecture

### Acquisition Sources
- Exploration
- Production
- Expedition
- Quest
- Encounter

No additional systemic acquisition mode is required.

### Recurring Resources
Common Materials:
- Timber
- Stone
- Metal
- Fiber

Special Materials:
- Essence
- Nectar
- Musk
- Estrus

Components:
- Arcane Component
- Organic Component

Unique Resources:
- Individually named and context-specific.

### Consumption Destinations
- Buildings
- Equipment
- Transmutation
- Specialized Content / Unlocks

### Productive Ecology
- Structural Flora
- Sensory Flora
- Transformative Flora
- Exceptional Flora
- Productive Organisms
- Structural Organisms
- Magical Organisms
- Exceptional Organisms
- Mineral Environments
- Magical Phenomena
- Sensory Environments
- Managed Community Ecology

The Wound modifies existing ecological relationships rather than constituting a parallel resource economy.

### Renewal Model
- Common: abstract renewable
- Special: cyclical, variable, or contextual
- Components: expedition-limited or production-supported according to type
- Unique: contextual

## Consistency Findings

### Finding 1 — No orphaned recurring resource
Every approved recurring resource has at least one ecological source and at least one meaningful consumption destination.
Status: PASS.

### Finding 2 — No mandatory new subsystem
Exploration, Encounters, Production, Expeditions, Quests, Buildings, Equipment, and Transmutation are sufficient to support the architecture.
Status: PASS.

### Finding 3 — Adult material culture is structurally integrated
Sexualized resources, organisms, environments, and adult buildings use the same acquisition and consumption architecture as the rest of the game.
Status: PASS.

### Finding 4 — Common resources remain simple
Timber and Stone remain primarily infrastructural; Metal and Fiber have limited additional roles.
Status: PASS.

### Finding 5 — Special resources have differentiated identities
- Essence: vitality, power, specialization
- Nectar: botany, cultivation, consumption
- Musk: sensory influence and specialized expression
- Estrus: fertility, cycles, transformation
Status: PASS, with future item definitions required to preserve these distinctions.

### Finding 6 — Wound economy remains controlled
No recurring dedicated Wound currency has been introduced. Traits and contextual Unique Resources handle exceptional cases.
Status: PASS.

### Finding 7 — Grind controls exist
Alternative sources, overlapping reward pools, renewable common materials, variable opportunities, and stable community production reduce forced repetition.
Status: PASS.

### Finding 8 — Region framework is reusable
Five productive profiles can support future exploration areas without requiring a complete biome catalogue:
- Settlement / Managed Landscape
- Wild Sensory Growth
- Living / Biological Territory
- Mineral / Ancient Territory
- Wound-Influenced modifier
Status: PASS.

### Finding 9 — Building proliferation has a filter
Resource-facing buildings should primarily provide Access, Stability, Conversion, or Specialization.
Status: PASS.

## Remaining Intentional Gaps
These are deferred content decisions, not architectural failures:
- concrete named resources and variants
- exact region roster and world map
- exact flora and organism roster
- exact buildings and their production recipes
- quantities, costs, rates, and balancing
- concrete expedition reward tables
- specific Unique Resources and quest chains
- presentation and terminology decisions for individual resources

## Explicit Scope-Creep Risks
Do not introduce without a demonstrated gameplay need:
- separate sexual inventory or economy
- universal processing/refinement tree
- detailed farming simulator
- permanent resource depletion simulation
- ecological maintenance chores
- dedicated Wound currency by default
- biome-specific currencies
- a separate management subsystem for adult buildings

## Final Verdict
The Resource and Ecology Audit is architecturally complete. The model is intentionally content-light but system-complete: future resources, regions, organisms, buildings, and adult material culture can now be added through established interfaces without redesigning the economy.

## Recommended Next Phase
Move from architecture to concrete content in this order:
1. Audit and define the building roster using the A9 four-function filter.
2. Populate concrete resource items only when a building, equipment piece, quest, encounter, or region requires them.
3. Define a small initial region roster using A8 profiles.
4. Connect concrete content to existing skills, combat rewards, Transmutation, and narrative progression.
5. Perform a cross-system integration audit once Buildings, Items, and regional content are populated.
