# Resource & Ecology Audit A4 — Sources and Acquisition Chains

## Purpose
Map approved recurring resources to the existing acquisition loops while avoiding grind, redundant acquisition systems, and long mandatory crafting chains.

## Core Rule
Prefer chains of two or three meaningful steps:
- Explore -> Obtain -> Use
- Obtain -> Process -> Use

Avoid long mandatory chains of repeated refinement.

## Existing Acquisition Sources
Do not add new systemic acquisition modes. Use:
- Exploration
- Production
- Expedition
- Quest
- Encounter

Cultivation, harvesting, extraction, and similar presentation-level activities belong to Exploration or Production unless a later design need proves otherwise.

## Acquisition Matrix

| Resource | Primary Source | Secondary Sources |
|---|---|---|
| Timber | Exploration | Production |
| Stone | Exploration | Expedition |
| Metal | Exploration | Expedition |
| Fiber | Exploration | Production |
| Essence | Encounter | Production, Quest |
| Nectar | Exploration | Production |
| Musk | Encounter | Production |
| Estrus | Encounter | Production, Quest |
| Arcane Component | Expedition | Encounter, Quest |
| Organic Component | Production | Encounter, Exploration |
| Unique Resources | Quest / Region | Expedition, Encounter |

## Resource-Specific Decisions

### Timber
Exploration is primary. Base production may provide a stable complementary supply. Use directly for construction and related infrastructure.

### Stone
Exploration is primary. Expeditions may return harder-to-access mineral variants. No mandatory refinement chain.

### Metal
Exploration and expeditions provide the systemic resource. Do not initially model Ore -> Ingot -> Metal as separate recurring currencies.

### Fiber
Exploration provides natural sources; production can process or maintain supply. Supports equipment, textiles, flexible construction, and related uses.

### Essence
Primarily obtained through encounters, phenomena, interactions, and special resolutions rather than being treated as a generic mined resource. Production and quests provide alternatives.

### Nectar
Anchors botanical ecology. Found through exploration and supplied through abstracted community production/cultivation rather than a standalone farming simulator.

### Musk
Primarily tied to encounters and world biology. Acquisition may result from cooperation, exchange, natural collection, events, or production; it is not inherently violent loot.

### Estrus
Primarily tied to encounters in the broad sense, including social interactions, biological cycles, creatures, and phenomena. Production and quests provide secondary paths. It is a progression material, not mandatory upkeep fuel.

### Arcane Component
Expeditions are primary, giving the expedition system a clear economic identity. Used by existing Transmutation and equipment empowerment loops.

### Organic Component
Production is primary, with encounter and exploration alternatives. Processing should remain simple and should not require a large recipe tree.

### Unique Resources
Use context-specific acquisition chains only when narrative or content requires them. Do not impose a universal refinement pipeline.

## Anti-Grind Rules
1. Activities should have overlapping reward pools rather than serving only one resource.
2. Recurring resources should normally have a principal source and at least one alternate source when appropriate.
3. Special resources should fund choices, upgrades, unlocks, transformation, or specialized production rather than constant upkeep.
4. Expeditions should complement active play and not block progress behind passive waiting.

## Result
Each existing gameplay loop gains a distinct but overlapping economic identity without adding a new acquisition system.

The next audit, A5, maps each resource to a small number of consumption destinations: Buildings, Equipment, Transmutation, and Specialized Content / Unlocks.
