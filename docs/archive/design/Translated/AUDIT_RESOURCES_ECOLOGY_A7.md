# Resource & Ecology Audit A7 — Renewal, Pressure, and Sustainability

## Decision
Adopt an abundance-oriented renewable model with variable opportunities rather than permanent depletion or punitive scarcity.

## Renewal Model
- Common materials: abstractly renewable.
- Special materials: renewable but cyclical, variable, or contextual.
- Rare opportunities: limited, rotating, or event-based.
- Unique resources: contextual.

## Rejected Models
Do not add:
- permanent depletion of individual trees or deposits
- permanently emptied mines
- mandatory ecological maintenance chores
- full agricultural calendars
- universal numerical depletion bars
- sustainability taxes or recurring punishment loops

## Common Materials
Timber, Stone, Metal, and Fiber are treated as region-level renewable availability rather than individually simulated world objects.

## Special Materials
Essence, Nectar, Musk, and Estrus may vary in availability through environmental conditions, biological activity, events, narrative progress, or abstract cycles.

Preferred source states:
- Dormant
- Active
- Abundant

States may affect rewards, encounters, events, or availability without requiring exact percentage displays.

## Community Role
Community production provides stability and predictability rather than mandatory upkeep. Managed sources can reduce dependence on variable exploration or encounters.

## Ecological Pressure
Use contextual state changes rather than a universal depletion meter:
- Normal
- Disturbed
- Altered

Pressure should create changed content, opportunities, encounters, or resource mixes rather than automatic punishment.

## Wound Rule
The Wound alters resource availability and ecological behavior. It transforms existing relationships rather than permanently deleting the world's productive capacity by default.

## Sustainability Rule
Sustainability is expressed through quests, events, upgrades, and ecological relationships, not recurring maintenance obligations.

## Renewal Matrix

| Resource | Renewal Model |
|---|---|
| Timber | Abstract renewable |
| Stone | Abstract renewable |
| Metal | Abstract renewable |
| Fiber | Abstract renewable |
| Essence | Cyclical / variable |
| Nectar | Cyclical / variable |
| Musk | Cyclical / contextual |
| Estrus | Cyclical / contextual |
| Arcane Component | Limited opportunity / expedition |
| Organic Component | Stable production / alternative sources |
| Unique Resource | Contextual |

## Result
The ecological economy supports exploration and community management without introducing survival-game scarcity or permanent economic soft-locks.
