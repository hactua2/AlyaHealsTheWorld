# Resource & Ecology Audit A8 — Productive Regions and Biome Framework

## Purpose
Translate the abstract productive ecology into a small reusable regional framework without committing to a large biome catalogue.

## Decision
Do not define every future region now. Adopt five productive region profiles that can be reused, combined, or presented differently through narrative and art.

## Productive Region Profiles

### 1. Settlement / Managed Landscape
Primary identity: community-managed ecology and reliable common materials.
Typical outputs: Timber, Fiber, Nectar, Organic Components, stable Essence/Musk/Estrus through production.
Primary loops: Base Production, nearby Exploration, community assignments.
Narrative role: demonstrates domesticated ecology and the material culture of pleasure, intimacy, reproduction, and transformation.

### 2. Wild Sensory Growth
Primary identity: dense sensory and transformative flora.
Typical outputs: Nectar, Musk, Essence, Fiber, occasional Unique Resources.
Primary loops: Exploration and contextual encounters.
Narrative role: strongly lewd environmental expression without requiring a separate mechanic.

### 3. Living / Biological Territory
Primary identity: productive fauna, magical organisms, biological cycles, and cooperative relationships.
Typical outputs: Musk, Estrus, Essence, Organic Components, selected Fiber.
Primary loops: Encounters, care, exchange, quests, and production relationships.
Narrative role: emphasizes consent-based interaction with living ecosystems rather than violent extraction.

### 4. Mineral / Ancient Territory
Primary identity: geology, ruins, old structures, and inaccessible materials.
Typical outputs: Stone, Metal, Arcane Components, occasional Unique Resources.
Primary loops: Exploration and Expeditions.
Narrative role: supports infrastructure and magical discovery while balancing the strongly biological resource economy.

### 5. Altered / Wound-Influenced Territory
Primary identity: an existing ecology transformed by the Wound.
Typical outputs: altered availability of normal resources, Wound-Touched traits, unusual encounters, and occasional Unique Resources.
Primary loops: Exploration, Encounters, Quests, and narrative resolution.
Narrative role: ecological conflict and restoration; this is a modifier-style profile and should usually borrow the base identity of another region.

## Important Rule
The Wound-Influenced profile is not automatically a separate biome. Prefer combinations such as:
- Wound-Touched Wild Sensory Growth
- Altered Living Territory
- Disturbed Mineral / Ancient Territory

## Region Composition
A concrete game region should normally select:
- one primary productive profile
- optionally one secondary influence
- zero or one major modifier such as Wound influence

Avoid stacking several unrelated profiles into one region unless narrative content specifically justifies it.

## Resource Coverage

| Profile | Main Resource Families |
|---|---|
| Settlement / Managed | Common materials, stable special production, Organic Components |
| Wild Sensory | Nectar, Musk, Essence, Fiber |
| Living / Biological | Musk, Estrus, Essence, Organic Components |
| Mineral / Ancient | Stone, Metal, Arcane Components |
| Wound-Influenced | Altered existing outputs, traits, Unique Resources |

## Scope Guardrails
Do not yet define:
- full world map
- region count
- species lists
- individual gathering nodes
- seasonal maps
- biome-specific crafting trees

## Result
The project now has enough regional structure to place resources and lewd ecological identity into future exploration areas without prematurely designing the entire world.
