# AHTW — Adult Character Situations & CG Matrix

## Status and scope

**Content planning reference — first-pass CG direction.** This document translates the current 31-character visual roster into three escalating, non-graphic adult/sensual situations and three CG beats per character. It complements, but does not replace, the character source-of-truth documents or `docs/art/CHARACTER_IMAGE_PROMPTS.md`.

All characters are adults. Scenes should communicate mutual agency, trust, personality and narrative consequence. Intimacy is implied through composition, affection, clothing state and aftermath rather than explicit sexual depiction. Do not assume that a character's presentation category determines sexual role or personality.

## Shared three-level structure

- **Level 1 — Tease:** flirtation, provocative pose, eye contact, playful tension, revealing but character-appropriate clothing.
- **Level 2 — Intimacy:** consensual kiss, close embrace, affectionate touch, vulnerability and partially disarranged clothing; the scene remains non-graphic.
- **Level 3 — Aftermath:** intimacy is implied to have happened; focus on emotional aftermath, tenderness, humor, changed trust or a consequential decision.

For each character, the three CG beats are:
1. **CG1 / Tease:** a visually readable flirtation or tension beat.
2. **CG2 / Intimacy:** a close two-character composition showing mutual agency.
3. **CG3 / Aftermath:** a quiet, humorous, vulnerable or story-relevant post-intimacy beat.

## Production rules

- Preserve the locked character identity, species traits, adult proportions, costume language and faction/profession palette from the Character Bible and image prompt pack.
- Sensuality must vary by personality; do not make every character gothic, fishnet-clad or performatively seductive.
- Clothing may be revealing, but should remain medieval/high-fantasy in construction and appropriate to the character's role.
- Keep consent and agency legible in the composition, especially for characters with histories of trauma, rehabilitation or manipulation.
- Level 3 should have a character-specific emotional or narrative consequence, not just a more revealing pose.
- **Cut-ins:** half-body or three-quarter framing for combat, flirtation and reaction.
- **CG1 / Tease:** full-body or three-quarter composition with readable body language.
- **CG2 / Intimacy:** medium framing, usually two characters, with clear faces/hands and consensual closeness.
- **CG3 / Aftermath:** medium or wide framing; prioritize emotional continuity, quiet humor, trust or changed behavior.
- Generate in sequence: CG1 to lock visual identity and tone, CG2 to establish the pair's visual language, then CG3 to carry the emotional consequence.
- Variants may change camera angle, lighting, costume state and secondary participant, but should not change the underlying scene's meaning without a narrative reason.
