# Alya Heals The World — Character Image Prompt Pack

## Purpose

This document is the visual-generation companion to `docs/characters/CHARACTER_DATA_ALPHA.md`.

The prompts below are intended for full-body character references, preferably **896x1152**, on a **solid white background**. Tags are deliberately written as a continuous sequence rather than split into semantic categories, because the target workflow is tag-oriented.

The visual direction is **adult, sensual high-fantasy**. Sensuality should reinforce the character's personality, culture, profession and narrative role rather than forcing the entire cast into the same "goth/fishnet" aesthetic.

All named characters are adults. Avoid child-coded proportions, school-uniform coding, chibi proportions or juvenile facial styling.

## Global negative prompt

`lowres, worst quality, low quality, blurry, jpeg artifacts, bad anatomy, malformed anatomy, bad hands, extra fingers, missing fingers, fused fingers, extra limbs, missing limbs, duplicate character, duplicate weapon, deformed face, asymmetrical eyes, crossed eyes, poorly drawn feet, floating objects, cropped head, cropped feet, out of frame, text, logo, watermark, signature, speech bubble, panel, border, frame, background scenery, complex background, room, landscape, crowd, child, teenager, young-looking, juvenile proportions, loli, shota, school uniform`

## Global generation guidance

- Composition: full body, head-to-toe visible, centered character, enough margin for ears/horns/weapons.
- Canvas: 896x1152 portrait.
- Background: solid white only.
- Default camera: eye-level, three-quarter view unless the character's concept benefits from a different angle.
- Prioritize silhouette, face, species traits, costume identity and readable equipment over excessive decorative detail.
- Fishnets, garter belts, piercings and tattoos are optional visual vocabulary, not universal costume requirements.
- Revealing clothing should remain medieval/high-fantasy in construction: leather, linen, silk, chainmail, robes, wraps, corsets, belts, tabards, ceremonial fabrics and fantasy armor.
- Avoid making every character black/purple/gothic. Palette and materials should follow faction, profession, species and personality.

# Scene / pose variant library

These are recommended reusable variants for future generation passes.

### Recruitment
- Eye-level three-quarter view.
- Character facing Alya rather than the viewer.
- Hands relaxed, equipment visible.
- Expression should communicate the character's initial attitude toward the community.

### Community work
- Full-body three-quarter view.
- Character actively performing their community function.
- Include one or two relevant props rather than a full environment.
- Examples: hammer, ledger, herbs, map, staff, sewing tools, training equipment.

### Expedition
- Slightly low dynamic camera.
- One foot forward.
- Weapon/tool ready but not attacking.
- Cloak, hair, tails and ribbons allowed to create movement.

### Combat reference
- Strong silhouette.
- Weapon clearly readable.
- Dynamic diagonal posture.
- Expression appropriate to role: disciplined for Guardiã, aggressive for Rival, focused for Caçadora, analytical for Erudita.

### Social / H-adjacent character portrait
- Eye-level.
- Relaxed shoulders.
- Direct eye contact.

### Group scene

### Emotional progression
For each character, generate at least three expression variants:
1. baseline personality;
2. trust / vulnerability;
3. confident intimacy.

The visual arc should change expression and posture more than species, body type or costume identity.

## Consistency rules

1. **Species must be immediately legible.** Do not let generic human anatomy erase minotauroid, beastfolk, elf, mycelia, angel, demon, dryad, lamia or other defining traits.
2. **Profession must be legible before erotic styling.** A viewer should recognize healer, hunter, merchant, researcher, priestess, dancer, commander, etc. from silhouette and props.
3. **Sensuality is character-specific.** A dancer can be openly performative; a priestess can be elegant and ceremonial; a commander can be severe; a shy character can be sensual through hesitation and eye contact.
4. **Do not make fishnets the default.** Use them selectively. The same applies to garters, piercings, tattoos and gothic motifs.
5. **Medieval/high-fantasy construction is mandatory.** Avoid modern bras, modern underwear-as-outerwear, contemporary office wear, schoolwear, tactical-modern clothing and contemporary streetwear.
6. **Adult proportions must remain stable across revisions.**
7. **White background is intentional.** Do not add rooms, scenery, gradients, decorative frames or environmental clutter to the base reference set.
8. **Full body is mandatory for the base sheet.** Head-to-toe visibility takes priority over dramatic cropping.
9. **Equipment should be consistent with gameplay.** Do not give every character a sword simply because it looks attractive.
10. **H personality should influence pose and expression, not replace the character's normal identity.** Sexualized presentation is a layer of characterization, not a substitute for characterization.

## Production recommendation

For the initial visual library, generate each character in this order:

1. neutral full-body reference;
2. personality pose;
3. work/combat pose;
4. social/H-adjacent pose;
5. expression variants;
6. only then create alternate outfits or more specialized H assets.

This order makes it easier to preserve character identity when later generations become more stylized.
