# Alya Heals the World — Recruitable Allies Roster

## Status
**C3 — Conceptually LOCKED.** This is the source of truth for the recruitable ally roster. Numerical balancing, exact skill definitions, operational recruitment scripting and detailed H-content implementation remain downstream work and do not reopen the conceptual roster unless a concrete system gap is discovered.

## Global rules
- Target: **10 recruitable allies**; 8–12 is acceptable only if later evidence justifies adjustment.
- All recruitable allies can participate in H-content.
- H-specialization is a **secondary dimension**, never a formal class.
- Combat/community classes are flavor labels; actual function emerges from stats, skills, aptitudes, equipment and development.
- Species influences natural attributes/aptitudes but does not hard-lock builds.
- Characters without natural aptitude take longer to develop a function.
- Allies are persistent special community members and may be assigned to community activities.
- **Expedition** = autonomous delegated community/NPC activity; Alya does not participate. An ally on Expedition is unavailable to the active party. **Sidequest** = direct player activity requiring Alya/player participation.
- Approximately 8–9 allies should be meaningfully combat-capable; 1–2 may be strongly community-oriented. No ally is deliberately useless in combat.
- Approximately 7–8 allies should be excellent community contributors.
- Community coverage is not a one-ally-per-facility checklist; generic NPCs, facilities and systems can cover functions.
- Functional overlap is allowed when execution and identity differ.
- Species diversity is desired without requiring ten unique species.
- Current presentation distribution: **4 feminine, 4 futanari, 2 femboy**.
- Visual silhouettes should vary substantially.
- Every ally has a **signature mechanic/identity rule**.
- Prefer a small number of deep relationships over a complete relationship matrix.

## Character details

### 1. First Ally / Generalist
- Pragmatic + curious; balanced aptitude.
- Found alone after losing their group and trying to survive.
- Signature: **Versatility** — low-cost function changes plus development redistribution.
- Starts with 2 skills.
- Progression: lower penalty for changing functions + redistribution.
- Strong relationship: Inadaptada.
- H attitude: adaptable and trust-oriented.

### 2. Guardiã
- Disciplined + protective + reserved; rigidity and over-responsibility are flaws.
- Vitality dominant, Strength secondary.
- Security + training aptitude.
- Signature: **Guard + Interception**.
- Starts with 3 skills + signature protection capability.
- Progression scales with protecting allies.
- Relations: Cuidadora + Rival.
- H attitude: reserved until trust.
- Visual direction: exceptional height, large horns, muscular/athletic build, relatively simple equipment.

### 3. Rival
- Competitive, ideological/pragmatic and confrontational toward Alya.
- Strength + Agility; crisis-management aptitude.
- Becomes an antagonist, leads a competing group and impedes community expansion before later cooperating/recruiting.
- Signature: **Fury + Momentum**.
- Starts with 3 skills + signature.
- Progression: damage + stronger while disadvantaged.
- Relations: Guardiã + Alya conflict.
- H attitude: provocative/competitive, reluctant to show affection.

### 4. Erudita
- Excessively curious, eccentric/obsessive.
- Intelligence + Perception; research + alchemy aptitude.
- Researches arcane magic, biology/alchemy and creatures.
- Found studying a dangerous creature; Alya investigates.
- Signature: **Study creatures** — stronger against creatures already studied.
- Starts with 3 skills + signature.
- Progression: creatures studied + community research.
- Relations: Artesã + Inadaptada.
- H attitude: curious/experimental; initially intellectualizes intimacy.
- **Must remain a pure mage**, not a hybrid healer/support role.

### 5. Cuidadora
- Practical/objective and somewhat authoritative; demanding of herself and struggles to accept that not everything can be fixed.
- Intelligence dominant, Vitality secondary.
- Health + recovery aptitude.
- Signature: **Diagnosis + Prevention**.
- Starts with 3 skills + signature.
- Progression: conditions removed + people treated.
- Relations: Guardiã + Devoto.
- H attitude: practical/comfortable, often takes control.
- Distinction: restores/treats harm; Guardiã prevents/mitigates; Devoto reinforces cohesion.

### 6. Artesã
- Goblin inventor; eccentric, chaotic, impulsive and prone to unfinished projects.
- Intelligence + Perception; construction + production + equipment aptitude.
- Found building something absurd and recovering materials from ruins.
- Signature: **Construction/modification outside combat**. Traps can exist but are not the defining identity.
- Starts with 3 skills + signature.
- Progression: projects + equipment modified + construction.
- Motivation: professional pride and desire to build something important.
- Relations: Mercador + Erudita.
- H attitude: playful/enthusiastic/impulsive.
- Strong dependency on equipment modification, including cursed equipment; must enhance the item system without invalidating item identity.

### 7. Caçadora
- Fox beastfolk; quiet/observant + provocative/playful.
- Bow; Agility + Perception.
- Ranged DPS + traps; damage identity is precision + explosive damage.
- Found during an expedition while investigating an unknown region.
- Signature: hunting/discovery; traps are offensive pressure, not dedicated control.
- Expedition value: special-location/event discovery + reduced risk, without becoming mandatory for every expedition.
- Starts with 3 skills + signature.
- Progression: expeditions + regions + creatures hunted.
- Relation: First Ally.
- H attitude: provocative/comfortable; intimacy as fun.

### 8. Mercador
- Feline beastfolk; charming/persuasive likeable trickster.
- Combat is intentionally low-relevance; community value is commerce/economy.
- Motivation: commercial empire; believes money solves many problems.
- Arrives as an itinerant merchant seeking opportunities.
- Signature: better prices/availability + money generation + commercial opportunities.
- Starts with 2 skills + community signature.
- Progression: volume + profit + routes/opportunities.
- Relations: Devoto + Artesã.
- H attitude: charming/manipulative socially, later vulnerable.
- Do not add combat power merely to make roster symmetry.

### 9. Inadaptada
- Rare/exotic species with poorly understood aptitude; shy, determined and insecure.
- Strange/inconsistent natural stat distribution.
- Can eventually fill any function.
- Community identity: individual interpersonal relations + negotiation, distinct from Devoto's collective cohesion.
- Optional sidequest recruit; rejected by a community, with potential noticed during a crisis.
- Signature: **Exceptional learning/adaptation** — develops multiple functions without losing previous progress.
- Starts with 2 skills + signature.
- **Initially underperforms but develops unusually quickly**; player discovers potential rather than receiving an overpowered ally.
- Progression: functions + skills + training time.
- Relations: First Ally + Erudita.
- H attitude: shy initially, gains confidence and discovers preferences/identity.

### 10. Devoto
- Faerie-like humanoid, human-sized; idealistic, highly empathetic and optimistic.
- Flaws: trusts too much, self-sacrifices, difficulty saying no.
- Vitality + Intelligence; moral/integration/collective mediation aptitude.
- Protection + buffs in combat; not a healer.
- Found helping people during an enemy attack/collapse/abandonment of a community.
- Signature: protection + buffs tied to moral/community identity.
- Starts with 3 skills + signature.
- Progression: people integrated + conflicts resolved + morale.
- Relations: Mercador + Inadaptada.
- H attitude: shy initially, comfortable through trust, difficulty prioritizing own desires.

## Cross-roster audit

Combat coverage is healthy without another dedicated melee DPS or another dedicated mage. Identities are: generalist flexibility, mitigation/interception, bruiser momentum, pure magic, treatment/recovery, tool/construction utility, ranged precision/exploration, low-combat economy utility, adaptive long-term generalism, and protection/buffs.

Community coverage is healthy across generalism, security/training, crisis management, research/alchemy, health/recovery, construction/production/equipment, expedition/exploration, commerce/economy, individual negotiation and collective cohesion.

Key non-redundancy distinctions:
- First Ally = immediate versatility; Inadaptada = unusually fast long-term learning.
- Guardiã = prevention/mitigation; Cuidadora = treatment/recovery; Devoto = cohesion/integration.
- Artesã = construction/modification/tools; Caçadora = ranged hunting/exploration.
- Erudita = pure mage specialist; do not add another mage merely for distribution.
- Rival = Fury/Momentum + disadvantage scaling + ideological conflict/crisis leadership, not generic DPS.
- Mercador's weak combat presence is intentional.
