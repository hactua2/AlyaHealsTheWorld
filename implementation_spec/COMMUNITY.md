# Community and Assignments

## Member lifecycle
Join -> Integrate -> Assign -> Gain experience -> Unlock specialization/opportunities -> Personal development.

## Work domains
ProductionAndEcology, ExpeditionSupport, CareAndRecovery, ResearchAndModification, CombatAndTraining, SocialAndSpecializedServices.

## Aptitude
Natural aptitude may improve learning speed or performance but never invalidates a member for another role.

Experience and learned aptitude are permanent. Reassignment never deletes prior expertise.

Present aptitude as a qualitative descriptor plus ordered rank.

Suggested record: domainId, experience, rank, descriptor, specializationTags.

Assignments can produce output, events, discoveries, relationships, conflicts, stories, or opportunities. Members are not merely percentage modifiers.

Exact capacity, concurrency, automation, and assignment timing remain configurable content decisions.
