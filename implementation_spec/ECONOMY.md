# Economy and Resources

## Resource families
CommonMaterial: Timber, Stone, Metal, Fiber.
SpecialMaterial: VitalEssence, BotanicalExtract, SensoryCompound, CycleReagent. These are implementation identifiers; presentation labels are content-layer concerns.
Component: ArcaneComponent, OrganicComponent.
Currency: Gold.
UniqueResource: individually defined contextual objects.

## Acquisition modes
Exploration, Production, Expedition, Quest, Encounter.

## Consumption destinations
Buildings, Equipment, Modification, SpecializedUnlock.

## Rules
- Keep recurring resources few.
- Prefer short chains: obtain -> use or obtain -> process -> use.
- Gold supports conventional trade and does not replace special requirements.
- No recurring resource may exist without at least one meaningful sink.
- Reward pools may overlap enough to avoid mandatory single-activity farming.

## Renewal
Common materials are abstractly renewable. Special materials are cyclical, variable, or contextual. Rare opportunities may rotate. Unique resources are contextual.

Do not simulate individual node depletion or universal upkeep taxes.
