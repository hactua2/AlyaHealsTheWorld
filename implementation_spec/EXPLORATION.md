# Exploration and Encounters

## Presentation
Use a hybrid model: strategic region/destination selection plus explorable maps and encounter resolution.

Hand-authored maps are reserved for story-critical or structurally unique content. Repeatable exploration and gathering may use procedural maps.

## Region profiles
Settlement, WildGrowth, LivingTerritory, AncientTerritory. A region has one primary profile, optional secondary influence, and optional major modifier.

## Encounter categories
Combat, Social, Ecological, Narrative, Discovery, Danger, Opportunity, SpecializedInteraction. Categories may combine.

## Repeatability
Unique encounters resolve permanently or transform into a new state. Repeatable activities use variable states, modifiers, or reward pools.

## Failure and retreat
Retreat is normally available. Failure should produce contextual lost opportunities, altered rewards, changed state, or future consequences rather than universal game-over punishment.

## Region state
EcologyState can be Normal, Disturbed, or Altered. State changes content and opportunities instead of applying a generic punishment tax.
