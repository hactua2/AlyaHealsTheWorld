# Items, Equipment, and Modification

## Categories
Resource, Component, Consumable, Equipment, Unique.

Resources, components, and consumables are stackable. Equipment and unique items remain individually identifiable. No encumbrance system is required.

## Equipment
Use few impactful slots. Optional specialized slots may unlock through progression.

Equipment must alter choices, role, capability, skill interaction, defense, or playstyle. Avoid pure percentage inflation as the default reward.

Older equipment remains relevant through alternate builds or modification/evolution.

## Modification system
Use one unified modification system.
Primary inputs are ArcaneComponent and OrganicComponent.
Modifications emphasize specialization and trade-offs.

Modification is reversible with meaningful but non-punitive cost. Support saved configurations when practical.

Build reset or reconfiguration occurs through defined facilities or progression moments with light friction; do not create permanent traps.
