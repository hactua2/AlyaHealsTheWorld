# Progression and Gating

Narrative milestones unlock new possibility layers: regions, resources, facilities, upgrades, capabilities, and content.

## Gate types
HardGate: blocks access when early access would destabilize pacing.
SoftGate: allows curiosity or partial interaction while withholding full access.

## Anti-overdevelopment policy
Players may optimize, stockpile, and experiment within the current layer. Future layers remain inaccessible until their milestone conditions are satisfied.

Advanced upgrades can require milestone flags. Some resources only appear in regions unlocked later, allowing requirements to be visible before their source is discovered.

## Visibility
Systemic requirements may be explicit. Narrative discoveries may remain partially hidden. Quest interfaces may preview major reward categories.

Avoid arbitrary global time limits and hidden mandatory pacing timers.
